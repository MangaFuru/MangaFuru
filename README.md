MangaFuru 是為 iPhone 與 iPad 打造的漫畫閱讀器。把漫畫、條漫和電子書收進同一個書庫，從上次停下的地方繼續閱讀。匯入自己的 CBZ 漫畫、EPUB 電子書與 PDF，或連接自己的 Komga 書庫。

MangaFuru keeps your manga, webtoons and ebooks together on iPhone and iPad. Build your library and pick up where you left off. Import your CBZ comics, EPUB ebooks and PDFs, or connect your own Komga library.

這裡是 MangaFuru 的公開問題回報入口。需要上手教學？先看[使用指南](https://mangafuru.github.io/zh-hant/getting-started.html)；遇到問題則可依照下方步驟回報。

This is MangaFuru's public issue tracker. For setup help, start with the [getting-started guide](https://mangafuru.github.io/en/getting-started.html). To report a problem, follow the steps below.

## 回報問題 · Report a problem

**中文**

1. 前往 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/MangaFuru/MangaFuru/issues)，按 **New issue**。
2. 選擇 **MangaFuru bug report** 模板，照欄位填寫。
3. 不確定要填什麼？先看 [支援頁](https://mangafuru.github.io/support.html)，它會幫你把版本、裝置與重現步驟整理好，並即時產生可貼上的草稿。

**English**

1. Open [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/MangaFuru/MangaFuru/issues) and click **New issue**.
2. Pick the **MangaFuru bug report** template and fill in the fields.
3. Not sure what to include? The [support page](https://mangafuru.github.io/support.html) helps you assemble the version, device, and repro steps, and builds a ready-to-paste draft.

## 送出前 · Before you post

- 包含 **App 版本、iOS / iPadOS 版本、裝置型號、發生位置、預期結果、實際結果與重現步驟**。
  <br><sub>Include the app version, iOS / iPadOS version, device model, affected area, expected result, actual result, and steps to reproduce.</sub>
- 若與來源有關，**只附公開的來源或 host 資訊**。
  <br><sub>If it involves a source, include only public source or host information.</sub>
- **不要貼**密碼、私密 token、完整 cookie、付費內容、個人資料或私密診斷資料。公開 issue 任何人都看得到。
  <br><sub><b>Never post</b> passwords, private tokens, full cookies, paid content, personal information, or private diagnostics. Public issues are visible to everyone.</sub>
- 截圖與診斷很有用，但**附上前請先檢查內容**。
  <br><sub>Screenshots and diagnostics help — but review them before attaching.</sub>

## 適合放在這裡 · What belongs here

- App bug、閃退、效能問題 · App bugs, crashes, performance problems
- 閱讀器行為問題 · Reader behavior problems
- 書庫、清單、設定、來源、Repositories 的流程問題 · Library, Lists, Settings, Sources, and Repositories workflow issues
- 可重現的問題 · Reproducible issues
- [MangaFuru 網站](https://mangafuru.github.io)的文件缺漏 · Documentation gaps on the MangaFuru site

## 不適合放在這裡 · What does not belong here

- 漫畫內容請求 · Manga content requests
- 來源推薦或來源清單 · Source recommendations or source indexes
- 繞過登入、付費、存取控制或地區限制的請求 · Requests to bypass login, payment, access controls, or regional restrictions
- 私密憑證、cookie、token 或付費內容 · Private credentials, cookies, tokens, or paid content

> MangaFuru 不代管漫畫內容。來源、檔案、帳號與存取權限都由使用者自行設定與管理。
> <br>MangaFuru does not host manga content. Sources, files, accounts, and access rights are configured and managed by the user.
